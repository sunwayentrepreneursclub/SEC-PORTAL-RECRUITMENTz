# Learning Portal videos: brief for Brand & Content

Each module on the Learning Portal has one short video. The member watches it, reads the guide, then takes a short check. This brief covers what to film, what each video must say, and how to hand it back.

Source: AWI Working Paper (Learning Platform, sections 11–13; video production rules, section 9).

## What a good module video does

- Teaches **one thing** and nothing else. If it needs two videos, make two modules.
- Is **5 to 10 minutes** maximum. Most should be 4 to 7.
- Says the **same thing as the written guide**, in the same order. The guide and the check are written by the content owner; the video must not contradict them.
- Shows real SEC work wherever it can: real events, real projects, real screens. Generic club footage (smiling groups, certificates, stock clips) is not allowed.
- Sounds like a colleague explaining how to do the job, not a welcome speech or a promotion.
- Never frames SEC as fun, networking or resume-building. The frame is high standards, ownership and becoming capable. High standards, **not burnout**.

## Technical and brand criteria

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Format | 1920x1080, 16:9, MP4 |
| Audio | Clear voice first. Use a lapel or shotgun mic. No echo, no background music over speech. |
| Lighting | Even, no harsh shadows on faces. Prioritise clear audio, lighting and editing over cinematic effects. |
| Captions | Burned-in English captions. Members often watch on mute. |
| Names | Name and title on screen the first time anyone speaks. |
| Evidence | Do not leave a talking head on screen for long. Cut to footage, screen recordings or simple graphics while the person speaks. |
| Visual style | Consistent SEC and Sunway treatment across every video: same fonts, colours, lower-thirds and end card. |
| Music | Only music SEC has the right to publish. If unsure, use none. |
| Permissions | No company logos, restricted university branding, locations or corporate footage without written permission. Get speaker consent and confirm correct names and titles. |
| End card | Last 5 seconds: "Now take the check" with the module name. |

## How to structure every video

1. **Hook (0:00 to 0:20).** Open with the module's scenario, or the mistake it prevents. No intro music, no logo sting.
2. **Teach (the body).** Walk through the guide's headings, one at a time, each with an example from real SEC work.
3. **Good vs bad (about 1 minute).** Act out or narrate the good and bad examples from the module.
4. **Close (last 20 seconds).** One sentence that summarises the module, then the end card.

## Videos needed now

### 1. The SEC standard (Level 1, SEC Core)
- **Length:** 5 to 7 minutes
- **On camera:** President or an ExCo member (the module owner)
- **Teaches:** What SEC expects from every member, and what members should not expect.
- **Content, in order:**
  1. Hook: the sponsor-deck scenario from the module. A teammate has gone quiet for three days and the lead hasn't asked. What do you do?
  2. The six standards, about 40 seconds each, with a real SEC example for each: ownership, reliability, quality, learning, communication, accountability.
  3. What you should not expect: constant instructions, titles without responsibility, guaranteed leadership positions, repeated deadline extensions, no accountability, lower standards because the club is voluntary.
  4. Good vs bad: tell your lead on Monday with a plan, versus saying nothing and asking for another extension on the deadline.
  5. Close: "High standards, not burnout."
- **Must not say:** that SEC is "highly professional" (show it instead), or promise titles or certificates.

### 2. How SEC builds people (Level 1, SEC Core)
- **Length:** 4 to 6 minutes
- **On camera:** AWI lead, or whoever owns the system
- **Teaches:** The path from selection to leadership, and who owns what.
- **Content, in order:**
  1. Hook: a new member's first week. What happens after they are selected?
  2. The lifecycle as a simple animated graphic: Attract, Select, Train, Deploy, Evaluate, Promote.
  3. The learning path: Know SEC, Know your role, Know how to execute, Know how to lead. Show the Learning Portal on screen.
  4. Who owns what, in one line: "Leadership owns standards. Departments own knowledge. AWI owns systems." Say what to do if a guide is out of date.
  5. How a module works: video, guide, good vs bad, scenario, check. Passing the check marks it complete.

## Videos to plan for, but not yet

These modules are drafts. The owners must finish the content first, because the video follows the guide. Do not film until the owner confirms the script.

| Module | Needs from the owner first |
|---|---|
| SEC vision and direction | The final SEC vision and why the standards exist |
| Structure, departments and projects | The finalised organisation chart, reporting lines and Project Lead model |
| Communication and weekly updates | Final rules, the weekly update format and the tools list (best as a screen recording) |
| Authority and escalation | The authority rules and escalation process, with two or three real cases |
| Department training (each department) | Each department writes its own and films its own, using the same criteria as above |
| Project, Project Lead and Leadership levels | Content from the project and leadership owners |

## Also in the working paper: the recruitment video

The main recruitment video (60 to 90 seconds, shown on the public recruitment site) has its own brief in section 9 of the AWI working paper: University / Student LIFE speaker, one external partner who has really worked with SEC, and the President. That is separate from these module videos.

## Handing videos back

1. Name the file `L1-01-the-sec-standard.mp4` (level, order, module name).
2. Upload to YouTube as **Unlisted** (or Vimeo). Do not post it publicly.
3. Send AWI: the link, the captions or transcript file, and who appears on screen.
4. The module owner reviews the video against the guide before it is published. AWI pastes the link into the module in the admin Learning portal tab.
5. Fixes after publishing: re-upload to the **same link** where the platform allows, so the portal does not need to change.
