    We reserve the right to refuse, cancel, or revoke registration where necessary, including in cases involving inaccurate information, fraudulent activity, or violation of these Terms & Conditions.

    2. Tickets & Passes

    Each ticket provides access according to the specific pass purchased. Access to certain sessions, workshops, networking events, or experiences may be limited based on ticket type, capacity, or additional registration requirements.

    Tickets may not be duplicated, resold, or used for commercial purposes without prior written permission from SEC.

    Attendees are responsible for reviewing the details and access included with their selected pass before completing their purchase.

    3. Ticket Transfers

    If you are unable to attend, ticket transfers may be permitted before the stated transfer deadline.

    To request a transfer, attendees must contact the SEC team and provide the required registration details. Additional conditions or fees may apply.

    Tickets may not be transferred after the designated deadline or once event credentials have been issued.

    4. Cancellations & Refunds

    All ticket purchases are subject to the applicable cancellation and refund policy.

    Refund requests must be submitted before the stated deadline. Processing fees or other non-refundable charges may apply.

    If an SEC event is postponed, rescheduled, or moved to an alternative format, purchased tickets may remain valid for the updated event.

    5. Event Changes

    An SEC event's programme, speakers, sessions, schedule, venue, and other details may change without prior notice.

    While we aim to deliver the published programme, SEC reserves the right to make reasonable changes where necessary.

    Such changes do not automatically entitle attendees to a refund.

    6. Attendee Conduct

    All attendees are expected to behave respectfully and professionally throughout the event.

    Harassment, discrimination, disruptive behaviour, or any conduct that compromises the safety or experience of others will not be tolerated.

    SEC reserves the right to remove individuals from the event without a refund if their behaviour violates these standards.

    7. Photography & Media

    Photography, video, and audio recording may take place during SEC events.

    By attending the event, you acknowledge that your image, voice, or likeness may appear in event-related photography, recordings, livestreams, or promotional materials.

    If you have concerns regarding media participation, please contact the SEC team before the event.

    If you have any questions regarding these Terms & Conditions, please contact us using the email below.

    This draft Privacy Policy explains how Sunway Entrepreneurs Club (SEC, “we,” “our,” or “us”) collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you interact with our website, events, and community services.

    We are committed to respecting your privacy and handling your data responsibly.

    1. Information We Collect

    We may collect the following types of information:

    1.1. Personal Information

    When you voluntarily provide information, such as when you register for events or membership, subscribe to our newsletter, or contact us through forms or email, this may include:

    • Name
    • Email address
    • Organisation or role
    • Location (city/country)

    1.2. Usage Information

    We may collect limited, non-identifiable information about how you use our website. This data helps us improve our content and community experience. It may include:

    • Pages visited
    • Event registrations
    • Referring links
    • Device and browser type

    2. How We Use Your Information

    We do not sell your personal data.

    We use your information to:

    • Manage event registrations and memberships
    • Send community updates, newsletters, and event communications
    • Respond to inquiries and requests
    • Improve our website, content, and services
    • Maintain the safety and integrity of the community

    3. Communications

    You may receive emails related to events you registered for, membership updates, and community news and resources.

    You can unsubscribe from non-essential communications at any time by using the link included in our emails.

    4. Sharing of Information

    We only share personal information when necessary and responsibly: with trusted service providers (such as event platforms and email services) who help us operate the community, or when required by law or legal process.

    All partners and service providers are expected to handle data in compliance with applicable privacy standards.

    5. Data Security

    We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. While no system is completely secure, we strive to use best practices to safeguard your data.

    6. Your Rights

    To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.

    Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

    • Access the personal data we hold about you
    • Request correction or deletion of your data
    • Withdraw consent for certain uses

    7. Contact Us

    If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or how we handle your personal information, please contact us using the email below.

    sunwayentrepreneursclub@gmail.com
