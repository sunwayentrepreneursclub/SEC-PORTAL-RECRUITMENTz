Every Score Means Something Specific

Conviction is not a vibe. It is a number earned by hard rules, the same ones every time.

8-10
HIGH CONVICTION

Requires trend alignment across two or more timeframes, momentum confirmation, and the debate agreeing on direction. Nothing gets a top score on vibes alone.

6-7
MODERATE CONVICTION

A clean setup with real structure behind it, but missing one of the strict alignment requirements. A minimum 1.5 reward-to-risk is still required to issue any directional call.

≤5
CAPPED

If the chart read and the AI debate disagree, the score is capped here automatically. Disagreement is never hidden inside a misleadingly high number.
